# Content policy

This covers the community blog in Text to Robot and Robot Studio. I moderate it alone.

## Allowed

Robots you designed, with a title, a short description, tags and links that help someone build or understand them.

## Not allowed

- Weapons, or robots meant to hurt people or animals.
- Hate, harassment, threats, slurs or sexual content.
- Other people's personal details (names, addresses, photos, accounts) without their permission.
- Spam, advertising, link farms, scams or malware.
- Work you do not have the right to share.
- Anything illegal where you or I live.

## What happens

- A word filter refuses posts with slurs and obscene words, and a post may hold at most three links.
- Anyone can report a post. After a few reports a post is hidden until I review it.
- I can hide, restore or delete any post, and block a connection from posting for up to two days.
- In review mode new posts wait until I approve them.

You can delete your own post at any time with the private link you got when you posted it.

To reach me about a post, open an issue at https://github.com/megazron/text-to-robot/issues with the post's address.
